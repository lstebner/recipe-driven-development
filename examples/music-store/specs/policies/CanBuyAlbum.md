## Purpose
Whether a listener can buy an album, already owns it, or can't get it at all. AlbumDetail decides
what to show from it, GetAlbum reports it, and CreateOrder enforces it, so all three must agree.

## Inputs
| input | type | notes |
|---|---|---|
| album | [[model:Album]] | |
| listener | signed-in listener | absent when signed out |

## Output
One of `buy`, `owned` or `unavailable`.

## Rules
- R1: `unavailable` when the album isn't listed ([[model:Album#listed]]), whoever is asking.
- R2: Otherwise `owned` when the listener is signed in and has a completed order for the album. A refunded order doesn't count.
- R3: Otherwise `buy`, including for signed-out visitors: they sign in when they press Buy.

## Examples
| case | inputs | output |
|---|---|---|
| signed out | listed album, no listener | buy |
| bought it | listed album, a completed order | owned |
| refunded | listed album, only a refunded order | buy |
| delisted after buying | unlisted album, a completed order | unavailable |

## Must not
- Never trust what the client says about ownership: it comes from the listener's orders.
